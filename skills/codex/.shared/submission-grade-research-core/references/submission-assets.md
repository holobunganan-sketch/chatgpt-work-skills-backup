# Submission and Revision Assets

Generate at least these files for a submission-grade package:
- `docs/manuscript.docx`
- `docs/abstract.docx`
- `docs/title_page.docx`
- `docs/cover_letter.docx`
- `docs/figure_legend.docx`
- `docs/highlights.docx`
- `submission/submission_checklist.md`
- `submission/file_manifest.md`
- `revision/reviewer_comment_matrix.docx`
- `revision/response_to_reviewers.docx`

For full original articles, enforce these manuscript expectations unless the user or target journal overrides them:
- main text from Introduction through Discussion should be approximately 4500 to 5000 words
- references should be at least 30 entries
- at least 85% of references should come from the most recent 5 years
- Introduction must contain exactly 3 medium-length natural paragraphs
- Discussion must contain exactly 5 long natural paragraphs
- Conclusion must contain exactly 1 medium-to-long paragraph

Discussion expectations:
- synthesize the main findings rather than merely restating the Results
- compare the findings with multiple strands of prior literature, not just one or two papers
- discuss plausible mechanisms or theoretical interpretation at appropriate depth
- separate strengths, limitations, and practical or research implications clearly
- keep causal language disciplined and matched to the design
- carry a substantial share of the manuscript length

Results expectations:
- carry a substantial share of the manuscript length
- report enough model-based and supporting analyses to sustain a submission-grade paper rather than an extended abstract

Table and figure expectations:
- all major tables and figures must be inserted into the manuscript body
- each table and figure must be numbered sequentially
- each table and figure must be explicitly cited in the surrounding body text
- each cited table and figure must have a corresponding interpretive discussion paragraph, not just a bare callout
- tables should appear in academic three-line-table style unless the target journal requires another layout
- figures should use a white background with black elements and grayscale-safe styling unless the user or journal explicitly authorizes color

Citation expectations:
- unless the user or journal specifies otherwise, use AMA 11th superscript numbered citation style
- Introduction and Discussion must contain in-text citation numbers
- do not leave citations only in the terminal reference list
- reference authenticity is the strictest requirement; verify publication existence and DOI correctness before finalization

Quality-control checks before delivery:
- research question is explicit
- design and analysis match the claim type
- data lineage is documented
- all reported results come from executable analysis or documented computation
- tables and figures match locked results
- references and DOI values are real when cited
- Introduction has exactly 3 paragraphs
- Discussion has exactly 5 paragraphs
- Conclusion has exactly 1 medium-to-long paragraph
- reference count and recency targets are met for full articles
- numbered table and figure callouts appear in the body and match inserted objects
- Introduction and Discussion contain in-text numbered citations
- the manuscript body uses black text only
- inserted tables visibly follow three-line academic styling unless a journal override is documented
- inserted figures remain black-and-white or grayscale-safe unless color approval is documented
- the Results section covers baseline analysis, group-comparison analysis, and modeling analysis when supported by the dataset
- conclusions stay within evidentiary bounds
- the submission package is complete

If any check fails, mark the limitation in the relevant artifact rather than inventing missing content.
