# Manuscript Gate

Use this gate immediately before final delivery.

## Content Gate

Confirm that the manuscript:
- answers a concrete research question
- reports full methods, not just a brief sketch
- contains a substantial Results section with multiple analytic components
- contains a developed Discussion section with literature comparison, interpretation, strengths, limitations, and implications
- discusses each major inserted table and figure in the surrounding prose
- reports results through a clear sequence of baseline analysis, group-comparison analysis, and modeling analysis when the dataset supports those layers

## Form Gate

Confirm that the manuscript:
- uses section subheadings with longer natural paragraphs
- avoids excessive one-sentence or two-sentence micro-paragraphs
- maintains a submission-grade academic tone
- avoids meta-language such as `demo`, `example manuscript`, `workflow`, `template paper`, or equivalent unless explicitly requested
- includes numbered table and figure callouts in the body
- includes AMA 11th superscript-style in-text numbered citations in Introduction and Discussion unless the target journal requires another form
- keeps manuscript body text in black only
- inserts tables in academic three-line-table style unless a journal override is documented
- inserts figures in restrained academic black-and-white or grayscale-safe style unless color approval is documented
- has exactly 3 Introduction paragraphs
- has exactly 5 Discussion paragraphs
- has exactly 1 Conclusion paragraph of medium-to-long length

## Quantitative Gate

For a full original article, confirm that:
- Introduction through Discussion is approximately 4500 to 5000 words
- references are at least 30
- recent references make up at least 85% of the list
- effect estimates and uncertainty are reported where appropriate
- the default citation style is AMA 11th unless the user specified another style
- reference authenticity has been explicitly checked and uncertain references have been removed or replaced

## Revision Gate

If any item fails:
- revise the draft
- rerun QC
- do not finalize until the output is materially closer to target
