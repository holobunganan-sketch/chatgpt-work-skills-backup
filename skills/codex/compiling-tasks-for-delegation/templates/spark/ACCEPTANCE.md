# Acceptance

## Acceptance Criteria
- Every required chunk is `PASS`.
- Every chunk contains recorded mandatory-verification evidence.
- No chunk crossed its Allowed Scope.
- No continuation occurred without the user-approval gate.
- {task-specific final criterion}

## Validation Procedure
1. Review `SPARK_MASTER_INDEX.md` status for every chunk.
2. Review each completed chunk's verification evidence.
3. Confirm final deliverables against `TASK.md`.
4. Escalate any judgment-based final acceptance item to the capable model.

## Failure Conditions
- Any required chunk is `BLOCKED`, `FAILED`, or `HIGH_MODEL_REQUIRED`.
- Mandatory verification is missing or failed.
- Evidence is insufficient to establish a required result.
- Scope or continuation rules were violated.
